● The long read

The Money Behind the Yard Signs: How LA City Council Candidates Launder Donor Intent

Investigation · 17 December · A six-month investigation

Investigation
The
Wilshire
Pipeline.
Nine firms, one mail drop, $2.1 million

The mail drop on Wilshire Boulevard

01 / 04

The consulting problem

The three firms listed on Brighter Futures LA’s disclosure — Pacific Grove Associates, Civic Reach Strategies, and a company called simply Trellis — were each paid between $90,000 and $130,000 for services described as “strategic communications consulting.” None of the three firms has a website.

02 / 04

Following the money

A PAC or campaign committee makes a large payment to a consulting firm. The consulting firm, in many cases, has no visible operations. The money enters the firm and does not reappear in any subsequent public filing.

03 / 04

The Wilshire pipeline

The mail drop on Wilshire Boulevard is registered as the business address for at least nine separate consulting entities that have received payments from political committees active in LA city elections over the past six years. Total payments to firms at that single address exceed $2.1 million.

04 / 04

What oversight looks like

The Ethics Commission publishes all filings online, which creates an appearance of transparency. The raw data is available. But the filings are designed to record transactions, not to explain them.
